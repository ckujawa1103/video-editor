Pocket Cut

      
A small video editor that runs entirely inside your browser. Your files are never uploaded — the whole video engine (ffmpeg, compiled to WebAssembly) runs on this device.

      
    
        
  • Clip — cut a section out as its own file.
    • 
        
  • Crop & rotate — drag the box, pick an output shape, and either trim the leftovers away or fill them with a blurred copy of the video.
    • 
        
  • Audio — save the sound as an MP3, strip it out, or drop a new track in.
    • 
      

      
Add it to your home screen and it works offline. Long 4K clips are slow — trim first, then crop.

      

      
      
Use this if the app looks out of date or an update did not take. It clears the stored copy, including the engine, and reloads — the engine downloads again next time.
